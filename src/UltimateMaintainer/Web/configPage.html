Ultimate Maintainer

Small maintenance jobs for your server. Each job runs as a scheduled task under Dashboard > Scheduled Tasks > Ultimate Maintainer. The log is in the plugin's data folder (logs/) and in the server log.

Partial watch cleanup

Items someone started and never finished stay in their Continue Watching for good. This cleans them up once they have not been played for a while. Each user is handled on their own.

Runs as the scheduled task Partial watch cleanup (daily 04:00). Use Preview first: the first run may change many items.
Counted from that user's last play of the item.
Some items have a resume point but Emby never recorded when they were played (often from older apps), so the timeout can't apply to them. Off: they are left alone and listed under Skipped. On: they are cleaned up on the next run, whatever their age.

What happens to them

Either way the item leaves Continue Watching. An item the user had already watched before (a rewatch they stopped) stays watched; only its resume point is cleared. Marking an episode watched lets Emby offer the next one; the next episode cleanup below tidies those up.

Next episode cleanup

Continue Watching also offers the next episode of every series a user has started, even shows they dropped years ago. This hides that entry once nobody has played the series for a while, the same as the apps' "Hide from Continue Watching". Nothing is marked watched, and the entry comes back as soon as the user plays the series again.

Runs as the scheduled task Next episode cleanup (daily 04:05, after the partial watch cleanup).
Counted from that user's last play of any episode of the series.

Old devices cleanup

Emby keeps every browser, phone and TV that ever signed in under Dashboard > Devices, forever. This removes the ones not seen for a long time. Removing a device signs it out; if it comes back, the user just signs in again. A device that is in use right now is never removed.

Runs as the scheduled task Old devices cleanup (daily 04:10). There is no Undo for this one: check the Preview first.
At least 7 days.
