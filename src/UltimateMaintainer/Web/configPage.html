Ultimate Maintainer

Small housekeeping jobs that run every night. Each one is off until you switch it on, and Preview shows what it would change before anything happens.

Partial watch cleanup

Items someone started and never finished stay in their Continue Watching for good. This tidies them up once that user has not played them for a while. Each user is handled on their own.

When Counted from that user's last play of the item.
What happens
Either way the item leaves Continue Watching. A rewatch someone stopped halfway keeps its watched mark; only its resume point goes.

Next episode cleanup

Continue Watching also offers the next episode of every series a user has started, even shows dropped years ago. This hides that entry once the user has not played the series for a while, the same as the apps' "Hide from Continue Watching". Nothing is marked watched, and the entry comes back when the user plays the series again.

When Counted from that user's last play of any episode of the series.

Old devices cleanup

Emby keeps every browser, phone and TV that ever signed in under Dashboard > Devices, forever. This removes the ones not seen for a long time. Removing a device signs it out; if it comes back, the user just signs in again. A device in use right now is never removed.

When At least 7 days. There is no Undo for this job.
